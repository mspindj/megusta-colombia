# Me Gusta Colombia — 30-Day Content Plan

## Psychological Triggers (PLFS Scored)

### 1. Loss Aversion — PLFS: +14
"What you DON'T know costs more than a guide."
- Travelers fear losing money/safety more than they desire discovery
- $40 taxi scam vs $17 guide = the guide pays for itself before you land
- Apply: every piece of content should reference a COST of not knowing

### 2. Authority Bias — PLFS: +13
"Built by locals who watched tourists make the same mistakes for years."
- Insider/local credibility is the product's core differentiator
- Military-tactical language reinforces operational authority
- Apply: "Our local intel confirms..." / "Field-tested by residents"

### 3. Social Proof — PLFS: +12
"2,847+ travelers briefed. 4.9 rating."
- At $17, the decision barrier is low — social proof tips the scale
- Apply: numbers, testimonials, "join X travelers who showed up prepared"

### 4. Availability Heuristic — PLFS: +11
Make the DANGER vivid and recent — taxi scams, drink spiking, tourist traps
- When people can easily imagine the threat, they act
- Apply: specific stories, not abstract warnings

### 5. Anchoring — PLFS: +11
Anchor against the COST of mistakes, not against other guides
- $40 taxi ride anchors $17 guide as cheap
- $51 for 3 guides anchors $37 bundle as a deal
- Apply: always show the "ignorance price" before the guide price

### Pillar 1: SURVIVAL INTEL (Searchable)

Loss aversion content — what tourists lose by not being prepared.
- "3 mistakes every first-timer makes in Medellin"

## Content Rules

1. **Every post must have ONE clear CTA** — cheat sheet, city guide, or bundle. Never "check us out."
2. **Lead with the cost, not the benefit.** Loss aversion > aspiration for safety products.
3. **Use specific numbers.** "$40 taxi" not "expensive taxi." "9 chapters" not "comprehensive guide."
4. **Tease, don't give away.** Each post is a SAMPLE from the guide. Show enough value to prove credibility, hold back enough to justify the purchase.
5. **No emojis in copy.** Brand tone is tactical, not casual.
6. **Carousels are the growth engine on IG.** Prioritize saveable, shareable carousel content over single images.
7. **Pinterest is the long game.** Every pin is SEO — optimize titles and descriptions for search.
