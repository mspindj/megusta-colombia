## 1. Airport to City — Don't Get Played

- **Use ONLY official taxi counters or apps** — InDrive, Uber, or Didi. Never accept rides from people approaching you inside the terminal.
- **Expected costs (airport to city center):**
  - Bogota (BOG): ~$8–12 USD
  - Medellin (MDE): ~$10–15 USD
  - Cartagena (CTG): ~$5–8 USD
- **Agree on price BEFORE getting in.** If there's no meter and no agreed fare, walk away.
- **Skip currency exchange at the airport** — rates are brutal. Withdraw COP from an ATM instead (Bancolombia or Davivienda give the best rates).

## 2. First 3 Hours — Survival Checklist

- [ ] **Get a local SIM card** at the airport — Claro or Movistar, $5–10 USD. Bring your passport. Data-only eSIM works too (Airalo, Holafly).
- [ ] **Download these apps NOW:**
  - InDrive (transport — you set the price)
  - Rappi (food delivery, pharmacies, groceries)
  - Google Maps (download offline maps for your city)
  - WhatsApp (everyone uses it — businesses, restaurants, even police tips)
- [ ] **Cash is king** in small shops and street food. Cards work in malls and restaurants. Carry COP $50,000–$100,000 for day 1.
- [ ] **Dress down** — no flashy watches, no jewelry, phone in front pocket. Blend in.

## 3. 5 Phrases That Save You Money & Trouble

| Spanish | Meaning | When to use |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| "Cuanto cuesta?" | How much is it? | ALWAYS ask before buying, eating, or riding |
| "No gracias, ya tengo" | No thanks, I'm good | Vendor/hustler deflection — firm but polite |
| "Me puede dar la cuenta?" | Can I get the check? | Restaurants — they won't bring it unless you ask |
| "Es seguro por aqui?" | Is it safe around here? | Ask locals/hotel staff, not strangers on the street |
| "No dar papaya" | Don't be an easy target | THE golden rule of Colombia — memorize it |

---

## 4. Red Flags — Walk Away Immediately

- Someone "helping" with your bags at the airport without being asked
- A taxi with no visible meter, no registered plate, or a driver who won't use apps
- "Special price just for you, my friend" in tourist zones (Getsemani, Poblado, La Candelaria)
- Anyone asking you to hold or carry something for them
- Invitations to "private parties" from people you just met
