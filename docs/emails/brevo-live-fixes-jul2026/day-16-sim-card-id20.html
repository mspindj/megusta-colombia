Logistics Intel // Connectivity

SIM card in 30 minutes of landing.

The short version: Claro at any airport arrivals hall, 25,000–35,000 COP for a SIM with 30 days of data. Tigo and Movistar also work, but Claro has the best coverage if you’re doing any day trips outside the city.
By airport

At El Dorado in Bogotá
The Claro counter is in the national arrivals section, not international. Walk past baggage claim, out the doors, turn left. About 4 minutes. There’s usually a line but it moves fast.

At José María Córdova in Medellín
Main hall before you exit. You’ll see it.

At Rafael Núñez in Cartagena
Smaller airport. Claro counter is near baggage claim, right side.
What they’ll ask

They’ll ask for your passport. That’s it. No Colombian ID, no local number needed.

If they try to add extra plans, you can say no. The basic 30-day data SIM is enough.

WhatsApp works immediately on the Colombian number. Rappi, Uber, and InDriver all require a local number to register — so this is the first thing to sort before you leave the airport.
