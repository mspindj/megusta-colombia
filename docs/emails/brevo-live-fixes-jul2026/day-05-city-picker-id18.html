Three cities. Very different first weeks.

The intel that works in Bogotá doesn't apply in Cartagena. The neighborhoods, the transport, the price expectations — all different. Here's the breakdown.
BOG-72H
Bogotá
"No dar papaya"

High altitude — cooler than you expect. Chapinero is where most nomads land first: co-working within walking distance, restaurants open past 9pm, safe to walk the main strips at night. Transmilenio costs 2,950 COP and gets you most places. La Candelaria is worth a day, not a week.

MDE-72H
Medellín
"El Poblado for week one"

El Poblado for the first week while you learn the city. Laureles if you're staying longer — quieter, cheaper, locals actually live there. The metro and cable car to Parque Arví on a Sunday is worth planning around. "El Poblado prices" are not real Medellín prices.

CTG-72H
Cartagena
"Cógela suave"

Old city and beach are two different trips. Getsemaní has the good restaurants now, not El Centro. The heat reorganizes your entire day — early morning and evening are when things happen. Midday is for shade and food.
