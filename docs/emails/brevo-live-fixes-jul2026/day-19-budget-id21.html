What a week actually costs.

A week in Bogotá, eating at local places and staying somewhere decent: 2,500,000 to 3,200,000 COP. That’s $580–740 USD, give or take.
Bogotá breakdown

The almuerzo — set lunch at most neighborhood restaurants — runs 15,000 to 25,000 COP. Soup, main, juice. That’s your cheapest and often best meal of the day.

Accommodation in a good area: 280,000–400,000 COP per night on Airbnb. It swings depending on when you book. Two weeks out, you’re at the low end. Week before, the high end.

Transmilenio is 2,950 COP per ride. Uber across the city is 12,000–20,000 COP. A week of transport mixing both comes to about 60,000–80,000 COP.
Medellín runs about 15–20% cheaper than Bogotá on most things. Cartagena runs 30–40% more expensive the moment you’re near the walled city.
