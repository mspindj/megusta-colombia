You’ll probably book El Poblado.

It’s what comes up first. It has the infrastructure, and it works. But if you’re staying more than five days, Laureles is the neighborhood most people wish they’d found earlier.
The practical part

From Laureles to El Poblado: 20 minutes by Uber, usually under 10,000 COP. Metro stop is Estadio. From there you reach Parque Arví, El Centro, or the airport without much planning.

Airbnb in Laureles runs 30–40% cheaper than El Poblado for the same quality apartment. The coffee shops have seats. Restaurants are mostly local — Mondongo’s, El Rancho — without the tourist menu markups.

The Jumbo supermarket is 7 minutes on foot from most of the neighborhood. That matters more than it sounds after a few days.
No famous landmark in Laureles. Most people I know who stayed a month in Medellín ended up there.
